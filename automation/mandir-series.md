# Mandir Itihas series (owner's request, 2 Nov 2026)

The owner wants the long videos to tell the history of famous temples of Rajasthan, and then the rest of
India, in **multiple parts**. Shorts should cover the same temples, because people search for them by
name. STORY.md (sections 1 and 5) says when to use this list.

## How a part is chosen
- Work through the temples in the order below. Each temple has 2-4 parts:
  1. **Itihas aur sthapana**: where the temple is, who built it and when, and how the worship began.
  2. **Pramukh kathayein / manyatayein**: the main legends, each labelled as a tradition.
  3. **Mandir, darshan aur utsav**: architecture, main deity, aarti, and the main fairs and festivals.
  4. (optional) **Yatra jankari**: how to reach the temple. Only facts that do not go out of date
     (district, nearest town or railway station). Never timings or prices, which change.
- To find the next part, read `automation/log.md`. Long slugs follow `<date>-long-<temple-slug>-part-<n>`,
  e.g. `2026-11-05-long-salasar-balaji-itihas-part-1`. Take the first temple that is not finished and
  write its next part. Finish one temple before you start the next.
- Title pattern: `<Temple> ka Itihas (Part n) | <Devanagari name> का इतिहास`, at most 60 characters in Roman.
  End each part with "agle part mein…" and name what comes next.
- Playlist: "Mandir Itihas" (for both the long parts and the temple Shorts).

## Accuracy (strict)
- Every fact (place, builder, year, dynasty) needs **two independent sources**. Good sources are the
  temple trust's official site, Rajasthan Tourism (tourism.rajasthan.gov.in), the district's official
  site (*.rajasthan.gov.in), the ASI, and established news organisations. Wikipedia is fine as a pointer
  to sources, never as the only one.
- Legends are always labelled: "manyata hai ki…", "lok katha ke anusar…".
- If the sources differ on a date or builder, say "itihaskaron mein mat-bhed hai" or leave it out.
  Never guess.
- The images are AI illustrations, not photos of the real temple. The description must carry the line
  "Chitra AI dwara banaye gaye hain (sanketik)". Never call them "asli darshan" or "live darshan".

## Sati temples: special legal rule (Rani Sati Dadi, Dhandhan Sati Dadi and the like)
India's **Commission of Sati (Prevention) Act, 1987** makes it an offence to glorify sati: supporting,
justifying or propagating it, or eulogising a person who committed sati. YouTube's policies also forbid
content that glorifies self-harm. So for these temples:
- Cover the temple, the worship of the goddess (for example Narayani Devi / Dadi ji as kul devi), the
  town, the architecture and the community's devotion.
- **The story may be told as history** (owner, 2 Nov 2026: "badhava nahi dena, unka story batana h").
  Tell it the way a history documentary would: neutral, factual and sourced.
  - Who she was, the period and the place, and that tradition says she became sati. Use **one or two plain
    sentences** for the event itself, labelled as tradition ("itihas/parampara ke anusar…").
  - **No** praise of the act: no "mahaan balidaan", "amar tyag", "sati ki mahima" or "sati hona punya hai",
    and nothing that presents sati as an ideal or something to follow.
  - **No** graphic or visual depiction: no fire, pyre or burning in the narration details or in any image
    prompt. Images show the temple, the goddess in a calm darshan form, the town and the devotees.
  - Put the devotion on the goddess and the temple (Dadi ji's kripa, the faith of the devotees,
    architecture), not on the act.
- **Mandatory disclaimer**, spoken briefly right after the event sentence and also in the description:
  "Sati pratha Bharat mein kanoon (Sati Nivaran Adhiniyam 1987) dwara pratibandhit hai; yeh video sirf
  itihas aur aastha ki jaankari ke liye hai, is pratha ko badhava nahi deta."
- Do not promote events the law restricts (for example any sati-related mela or procession).
