`automation/festivals.json` (`node scripts/status-engine.mjs calendar`): edit that file, not the block below. Sources:
D = drikpanchang.com (New Delhi, Smarta, Purnimanta), P = prokerala.com with loc=1261481 (New Delhi); URLs in
festivals.json "sources". Rows marked "no status Short" are for the story writer only. Not in the window (after 31 Jan

Status Shorts (scripts/status-engine.mjs, scripts/day-plan.mjs): `eve` = public 20:00 IST the evening before,
`status` = 00:30 IST on the day, `statusday` = 08:30 IST (big festivals). A variant the 3-a-day cap drops is not made.
