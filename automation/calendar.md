D = drikpanchang.com (New Delhi, Smarta, Purnimanta), P = prokerala.com with loc=1261481 (New Delhi); URLs in
festivals.json "sources" (Holi 2027: D + timeanddate.com, Prokerala differs; Khatu Phalgun mela: khatu.org). Rows
marked "no status Short" are for the story writer only. The window runs to 31 Mar 2027 (Feb-Mar 2027 rows checked 20 Nov
