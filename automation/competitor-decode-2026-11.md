# Competitor decode: faceless devotional Shorts channels like Shyam Premi SN (20 Nov 2026)

Read-only research with the YouTube Data API v3 (the channel's own OAuth token, read calls only; nothing was uploaded,
liked, commented or subscribed). Quota used: **1,253 units** (11 searches x 100 = 1,100, one of them lost to a script
path bug and re-run; 153 list calls x 1). Data: 10 searches (khatu shyam status, shyam baba bhajan status, khatu shyam
darshan live, hanuman ji status shorts, mahadev status shorts, radha krishna status, ekadashi status, aaj ka darshan,
salasar balaji darshan, rani sati dadi; region IN, Hindi, Shorts length, published after 1 Jul 2026) gave 479 videos
from 211 channels; 15 channels were picked; for each: the last 50 uploads, the last 100 Shorts and the 50 most popular
Shorts (2,085 Shorts). Pattern maths use the **1,307 recent Shorts at least 7 days old**. The 164 channels already in
`automation/viral-radar/channels.json` are mostly katha/facts channels; only 2 were in this niche, so the searches
found the rest. Limits are at the end (we cannot watch videos, hear the audio or see thumbnails).

## Owner summary (Hinglish)

- **Sabse badi baat: festival status ka season pehle shuru hota hai.** Khatu Shyam Janmotsav (20 Nov) ke liye
  SHYAM STATUS VK ne "Mere Pyare Baba Ka Jannam Din Aa Gaya" **12 din pehle** (1.6 L views, 8.9x apne median se) aur
  **6 din pehle** (2.1 L, 11.2x) daala; BhaktiTube (sirf 8,500 subs) ka "Khatu Shyam Birthday Status 2026" **4 din
  pehle** = 2.1 L; Baba Shyam Status ka 7 din pehle = 3.3 L. Humne pehla Janmotsav status sirf 2 din pehle (18 Nov) daala.
  Phalgun mela ka sabse bada status (Prabhu Shree Guruji, 22 L) mela se ~3 hafte pehle aaya tha.
  **Rule:** Khatu ke bade din (Janmotsav, Phalgun mela) = 10-14 din pehle se countdown status; baaki tyohar = ek din
  pehle shaam (18-23 baje) + usi din subah 00:30-07:00.
- **Title formula jo chalta hai:** English pehle, phir Hindi: "Happy Bhai Dooj Status ✨ | Bhai Dooj WhatsApp Status"
  (BhaktiTube 4.3 L on 8.5 K subs). Tyohar ka naam title mein = **1.20x** (BhaktiTube ke andar 7.6x, Daily Best Wish 2.2x).
  Hindi se shuru hone wale title zyadatar channels mein kamzor (10 mein se 7 channels: 0.4-0.7x). Humara current
  "<Festival> Status 2026 | <हिंदी> स्टेटस वीडियो #Shorts" sahi disha mein hai; "Happy" + "WhatsApp Status" jodna hai.
- **Length:** 11-15 s sabse achha (median 1.09x, 16% Shorts 3x+). Humare 12-20 s status loops theek hain. Asli darshan
  channels 22-35 s (Shree Shyam Baba) ya 59 s aarti (Khatu Shyam Ji Mandir channel).
- **Timing:** subah 05-07 baje (1.08x) aur dopahar 14-16 (1.09x) achhe; **08-10 baje sabse kamzor (0.89x)**; tyohar ke din
  08-11 baje ka status 0.78x. Humara 08:30 "statusday" variant aur 09:30 owner-footage slot isi kamzor ghante mein hain.
- **Asli darshan (owner footage) ke liye model:** "आज के खाटू श्यामजी के लाइव दर्शन | 13 August 2025" (Shree Shyam Baba,
  30 L) aur "खाटू धाम से प्रातः आरती के लाइव दर्शन" roz 07:00 baje, ek hi title roz (median 14,800 views on 44,700 subs).
  Matlab: **ek fixed naam + taareekh, roz ek hi samay, subah aarti ke time.** Lekin hum "LIVE" nahi likhenge (recorded
  hai) aur "Khatu Dham" nahi likhenge (pandal ki footage hai).
- **Kya copy nahi karna:** in channels ke zyada tar hits **dusron ke gaane** par hain (Kanhiya Mittal, Hansraj Raghuwanshi,
  Chotu Singh Rawna; "Copyright Disclaimer Section 107" likh kar), SHYAM STATUS VK ka 9.2 Cr wala Short **Motu Patlu
  cartoon** hai, Hare Krishna Art ke hits **Premanand ji / Indresh ji ki awaaz** par hain, 80-90 hashtags (YouTube 60
  se zyada par saare hashtags ignore karta hai), "LIVE" recorded video par. Ye sab hum kabhi nahi karenge.
- **Kya copy karna:** formats: tyohar status (English-first title), Khatu countdown, roz ke dated darshan, "अर्जी कैसे
  लगाते हैं?" jaise how-to (1.29 Cr), mandir ka sach/ajooba (Sanwariya Seth bhandar, 2.46 Cr), bhakt ke dil ki ek line
  ("किसी और से क्या मांगू 🥺", 5.3 L) — humari apni line aur humara apna bhajan/music.
- Neeche: channel table, 15 patterns, timing, lead time, **20 Short ideas (21 Nov - 20 Dec)** with frame-0 text, aur
  viral-playbook.md ke liye **5 proposed edits** (apply nahi kiye).

## 6. What to copy vs what not to copy

**Copy (formats, in our own words, art, voice and music):**
- English-first festival status title with "Happy" + "WhatsApp Status" + the Hindi name (pattern 1).
- Khatu big-day countdown status from 10-14 days before (pattern 2-3).
- A daily real-darshan routine for the owner's footage: one fixed title + date, same hour, 20-35 s (patterns 4-5).
- Ritual how-to and verified temple facts as darshan/facts hooks (patterns 6-7): "अर्जी कैसे लगाते हैं?", "नारियल क्यों बांधते हैं?".
- One-line bhakt feeling over our own bhajan in `loop` Shorts (pattern 8, 10).
- Day-N series on multi-day festivals (pattern 13): Navratri, Chhath days, Phalgun mela days.
- The deity's own weekday only (Somvar Mahadev, Mangalvar Hanuman), not "सुप्रभात" on every day (pattern 11).

**Do not copy:**
- **Copyrighted songs**: the bhajan-line status channels run on singers' bhajans (Kanhiya Mittal, Sanjay Mittal, Hansraj
  Raghuwanshi, Chotu Singh Rawna, Vijay Rajput named in descriptions) with "Copyright Disclaimer Section 107"; a rap
  song (46.6 L) on Khatu Shyam Official Channel. Our bhajans stay our own AI-sung originals through the bhajan QA gate;
  library tracks stay licence-checked.
- **Copyrighted characters**: SHYAM STATUS VK's 9.24 Cr Short uses Motu Patlu.
- **Real people's voices**: Hare Krishna Art's top Shorts are Premanand ji / Indresh ji clips.
- **Other people's footage**: re-cut temple aarti/darshan clips, a stranger's parikrama clip ("धन्य है ऐसी बहू!").
  Only the owner's own footage and licence-checked photos.
- **"LIVE" on recorded video** and "Khatu Dham se" on pandal footage: misleading; we say "पंडाल से दर्शन" / "श्रृंगार दर्शन".
- **Miracle / money claims**: "करोडो रुपए", "बाबा ने खुद ...", "मनोकामना पूरी होगी" - only verified facts with a source,
  folk belief labelled "मान्यता/लोककथा".
- **80-90 hashtags, keyword-stuffed descriptions, other channels' names**: spam signals (YouTube ignores all hashtags
  above 60; validate.mjs already blocks other channels' names).
- Off-topic uploads (film promos, Independence/Mother's Day greetings): no feed identity.
- Rani Sati: competitors post mela/amavasya bhajans; our sati rules stay (darshan/bhakti only, the Sati Nivaran
  Adhiniyam 1987 disclaimer, no mela/yatra/amavasya, no fire imagery, no praise of the act, AI voice only).

## 9. Limits

- We cannot watch the videos, hear the audio, see thumbnails or read captions: art style, song use and "real vs AI" are
  inferred from titles, descriptions and tags only. Song use is certainly higher than the descriptions admit.
- Views are a snapshot on 20 Nov 2026. Recent Shorts (the Khatu Janmotsav and Tulsi Vivah ones) are still growing.
- Channels were picked from search results (Hindi, region IN, Jul-Nov 2026), so channels that search ranks well are
  over-represented. "Most popular Shorts" come from YouTube's popular-uploads playlist; the pattern maths use only the
  last 100 Shorts (at least 7 days old) to avoid that selection bias.
- Shorts were identified by length (at most 180 s, not live); a few horizontal clips under 3 min may be included.
- Lifts are correlation, not proof, and several groups are small (questions n = 22, AI n = 7). Festival dates before
  11 Nov come from the competitors' own titles (e.g. "11 अक्टूबर 2026 नवरात्रि", "Papankusha 22 October") and our
  logs (Dhanteras 6 Nov, Choti Diwali 7 Nov, Diwali 8 Nov); Nov dates from festivals.json.
- Retention, "viewed vs swiped" and traffic sources of other channels are not available; only views/likes/comments.
