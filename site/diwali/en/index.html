How to make your card

  1. Type your name. Add the name of the person it is for (Mummy, Rahul, the Sharma family), or leave it empty for a card you can send to everyone.
  2. Optionally tap Add a photo and pick a circle, arch or diya frame. Drag the photo on the card to fit it.
  3. Pick the festival and the card language: Hindi, English or Hinglish.
  4. Tap a design under the preview and Next message until the wish feels right, or write your own.
  5. Choose Status 9:16 (1080 × 1920) for WhatsApp and Instagram status, or Post 1:1 (1080 × 1080) for a feed post or a family group.
  6. Tap Share on WhatsApp, or download the PNG or the 5-second video with flickering diyas.

Questions

How do I make a Diwali greeting card with my name?

Type your name, and if you like the name of the person you are sending it to. Choose the festival (Dhanteras, Choti Diwali, Diwali, Govardhan Puja, Bhai Dooj or a general Happy Diwali), the language and one of the ten designs. Tap Next message until you like the wish, or write your own, then download the card as a PNG image or a short video.

How do I share the card on WhatsApp or put it on my status?

On an Android phone or iPhone, tap Share on WhatsApp and pick WhatsApp from the share sheet: the card goes as an image with the wish and a link. For a status, choose the Status 9:16 size, download the PNG or the video, then add it from your gallery in the Status tab of WhatsApp. On a computer the button opens WhatsApp with the message and a link to the card.

Is the Diwali card maker free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark across the card and no limit on how many cards you make. A small Shyam Premi SN mark with the YouTube handle sits in one corner.

Are my name, photos or cards stored anywhere?

No. The card is drawn in your browser. Your names and any photo you add stay on your phone: the photo is only drawn onto the card on your device, it is never uploaded, and no copy is kept. A shared link only carries the names in the link itself (never the photo), so the person who opens it sees the card made for them.

Can I add my own photo to the card?

Yes. Tap Add a photo, take a picture or pick one from your gallery, and choose a circle, arch or diya-shaped frame. Drag the photo on the card to move it and use the zoom slider (or pinch) to fit your face. You can add a second photo for a couple or family card.

Is there a Jai Shree Shyam Diwali card?

Yes. Choose Jai Shree Shyam: Shubh Deepawali as the festival for devotional wishes for Khatu Shyam ji bhakts, and pick one of the three Shyam designs with peacock feathers and marigold garlands.

Can I write the wishes in Hindi?

Yes. Choose हिन्दी for wishes in Devanagari, English, or Hinglish (Hindi written in English letters). You can also type names in Hindi; the card uses Noto Sans Devanagari, so joined letters and matras display correctly.

Why does the video download as WebM on my computer?

The video is recorded by your browser. Recent Chrome, Edge and Safari versions save MP4, which WhatsApp accepts directly; older browsers save WebM. If WhatsApp will not take the file, use the PNG instead.
